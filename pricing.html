Pricing & Payment FAQs

Everything you need to know about our billing and setup process.

How do I make the payment?

Once you click 'Choose Plan', you will be redirected to our Secure Client Portal. After logging in, you can pay instantly via UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) and submit your UTR reference securely.

Are there any hidden hosting fees?

No. We deploy your website on high-speed global CDNs. The price listed is a one-time fee for design and deployment. If you require a custom `.com` or `.in` domain, we can guide you on purchasing it separately at cost price.

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Absolutely. If you start with the Starter Plan and later wish to add more pages, SEO, or animations, you can request an upgrade directly from your Client Dashboard at any time.
