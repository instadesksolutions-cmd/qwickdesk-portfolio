Terms of Service

Clear, transparent, and fair rules for our digital partnership.

1. Payment & Billing Terms

  • Standard Packages: Deliverables and payments strictly follow what is stated in your chosen pre-defined plan.
  • Custom Projects: A non-refundable upfront advance of ₹899 is required. The remaining balance must be cleared upon completion.

2. Default & Project Handover

For custom projects, the final website, credentials, and source code will only be handed over once the final payment is cleared. If the client fails to pay, the advance of ₹899 is non-refundable, and QwickDesk Solutions reserves the right to withhold the website.

3. Maintenance & Technical Support

  • Standard Packages: Free technical support is provided per your pricing plan.
  • Custom Projects: Includes 60 Days of free technical support (bug fixes) from the live date. New features are billed separately.

4. Client Responsibilities

Clients must provide assets (logos, text, images) on time. Excessive delays may extend project timelines.

5. Intellectual Property

Upon full clearance of dues, ownership transfers to the client. We reserve the right to showcase it in our portfolio.
